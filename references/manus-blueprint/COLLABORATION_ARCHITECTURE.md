# CASPA Collaboration, Style, and Blind Review Policy

## Author-owned style library

CASPA accepts only **author-owned or explicitly licensed** style samples. The author must confirm this at creation, can retain a private source note, and may export or remove any sample at any time. CASPA stores the sample as a project-private object and never labels a profile as another living or named author’s style.

The generated profile is a compact, non-identifying description of craft: point of view, sentence rhythm, dialogue density, imagery, pacing, register, lexical texture, and editorial cautions. It does not include verbatim source text in prompts or author-facing results. A style profile is optional, versioned, and may only influence a generation when the author selects it in the draft brief.

## Collaboration roles

| Role | Scope | May do | Cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Author** | Owner | Invite, revoke, manage roles, select style profiles, accept drafts, set approval requirements, approve every consequential change. | — |
| **Editor** | Project-scoped | Read project material, add proof comments, join blind review, submit editorial feedback, approve revisions only when the author enables that gate. | Invite others, alter owner settings, accept drafts, delete project data, approve design assets. |
| **Designer** | Project-scoped | Read approved manuscript/art direction, comment on proofs, submit visual feedback, approve covers/illustrations/layout only when the author enables that gate. | Access private style samples, invite others, alter manuscript, accept drafts, delete project data. |

Invites are address-bound and must be accepted by a signed-in account with the invited email. A revoked collaborator is excluded from all subsequent queries and protected mutations. The author remains the only actor who can archive or delete a project.

## Blind editorial reviews

The author starts a review round from a fixed manuscript version. CASPA creates a random anonymous label and hides author/contributor identifiers in reviewer-facing data. The submitted manuscript selection is immutable while the round is open. Reviewers provide scored feedback and optional narrative feedback; submissions cannot be edited after submission. Authors see aggregate scores during an active round. Reviewer identities are disclosed only after closure when the author explicitly selects an identity-visible policy.

## Approval and audit model

Author approval remains mandatory for all consequential changes. The author may additionally require an editor approval for revision plans and a designer approval for cover, illustration, layout, proof, or production export. Every invitation, role change, review submission, decision, revocation, and approval is recorded in a project audit event. A collaborator’s approval is never sufficient by itself to release manuscript or production work.
