# CASPA Visual Direction

CASPA is a **private literary atelier** rather than a conventional software dashboard. The interface uses ink-black and deep-slate surfaces, warm ivory type, antique-brass accents, a high-contrast editorial serif for authored moments, and a quiet humanist sans for controls. Layouts should evoke a manuscript desk through folio cues, rules, margin-note composition, measured whitespace, and subtle paper-depth surfaces rather than decorative gradients or neon technology motifs.

The brass accent is disciplined: it identifies the primary action, active project state, selected editorial finding, and critical proofing marks. It should not decorate every label. Rounded panels remain restrained and tactile, with thin rules, low-contrast archival texture, and warm depth.

CASPA’s voice is calm, editorial, and precise. Copy should sound like a trusted manuscript partner guiding the author to one clear next action. Avoid generic productivity language, exaggerated AI claims, infrastructure jargon, and model/provider terminology.
